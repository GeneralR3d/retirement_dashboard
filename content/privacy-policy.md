## 2. Commitment to Your Privacy

We take your privacy seriously. Retirement.SG is designed with privacy by default: the Website does **not** require account registration, does **not** collect personally identifiable information such as your name, NRIC, or address, and does **not** transmit any financial data you enter into its calculators to our servers.

All calculation inputs (salaries, CPF balances, expenses, etc.) are stored exclusively in your browser's `localStorage` on your own device. This data never leaves your browser unless you explicitly copy or export it.

## 3. Information We May Collect

### 3.1 Automatically Collected Data (Log Data and Analytics)

When you visit the Website, certain information is automatically collected by our hosting infrastructure and third-party analytics tools. This may include:

- Your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address (typically anonymised or truncated before storage)
- Browser type and version
- Operating system
- Referring URL
- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- Date and time of your visit
- Approximate geographic region (country or city level, derived from IP)
- Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

This information is collected in aggregate and is used solely to understand how the Website is used, diagnose technical issues, and improve the Service. It is **not** used to identify you as an individual.

### 3.2 Calculator Inputs

Any data you enter into Retirement.SG's calculators — including salary figures, CPF balances, expenses, age milestones, and investment values — is stored locally in your browser via `localStorage`. **We do not receive, transmit, or store this data on any server we operate.** If you clear your browser's local storage or use a private/incognito window, this data will be lost.

### 3.3 Contact and Feedback

If you contact us voluntarily (for example, to report an error or provide feedback), we will receive only the information you choose to share — typically your email address and the content of your message. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not shared with third parties except as required by law.

## 4. Google Analytics and Usage Tracking

The Website uses **Google Analytics**, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC ("Google"). Google Analytics uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to collect and report information about how visitors use the Website.

The information generated by cookies about your use of the Website (including a truncated version of your IP address) is transmitted to and stored by Google on servers which may be located outside Singapore. Google will use this information on our behalf to evaluate your use of the Website, compile reports on Website activity, and provide other services relating to Website activity and internet usage.

- We have enabled **IP anonymisation** in Google Analytics so that your IP address is truncated before storage.
- Google Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- You may also manage your personalised advertising preferences via [Google's Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

We may in future introduce additional analytics or error-monitoring tools. This Policy will be updated accordingly, and those tools will be identified here.

## 5. Cookies

### 5.1 What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website. They allow the website to remember information about your visit.

### 5.2 How We Use Cookies

**Currently**, Retirement.SG does not set any first-party cookies for functionality purposes. All user preferences and calculator data are stored in `localStorage`, not cookies.

However, third-party services we use (including Google Analytics) may set their own cookies on your device to perform analytics functions as described in Section 4. These are analytics cookies only and are not used to track you across unrelated websites for commercial advertising purposes.

**In the future**, we reserve the right to introduce:

- First-party session or preference cookies to improve your experience;
- Cookies associated with advertising or sponsored content (see Section 8 on Future Advertising).

Any future introduction of cookies beyond the current analytics cookies will be reflected in an updated version of this Policy.

### 5.3 Managing Cookies

Most web browsers automatically accept cookies. You may modify your browser settings to decline cookies or to alert you when a cookie is being sent. Please refer to your browser's help documentation for instructions.

Note that declining certain cookies may affect your ability to use some features of the Website or third-party analytics services.

## 6. Local Storage and Browser Data

Retirement.SG uses the browser's `localStorage` API to persist your calculator inputs between sessions on the same device and browser. This data:

- Is stored only on your device;
- Is not accessible to us or any third party;
- Can be deleted at any time by clearing your browser's site data or local storage.

If you use the Website across multiple devices or browsers, your data will **not** automatically synchronise, as there is no server-side account or storage.
